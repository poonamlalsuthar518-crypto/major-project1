Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the safe navigation feature?
Enter your source and destination, then choose the safest route from the list of generated recommendations.
Can I report an incident anonymously?
Yes, you can enable the anonymous checkbox while filing a CivicSense report.
What should I do in an emergency?
Press the SOS button, call emergency services, and share your live location using the emergency actions.
