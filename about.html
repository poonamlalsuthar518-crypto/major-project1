About SecureStep

Smart personal safety and navigation for modern cities

Project Overview

SecureStep is a safety-first urban mobility platform designed to help students, commuters, and professionals navigate cities with confidence. It combines route safety intelligence, community reporting, emergency response support, and real-time visualization.

Objectives

  • Improve situational awareness during travel.
  • Provide safer route recommendations.
  • Encourage local reporting and prevention.
  • Support emergency communication and response.

Features

  • Smart route navigation with safety scoring
  • Crime heatmap and hotspot detection
  • Civic reporting and anonymous incident submissions
  • Emergency SOS and contact management
